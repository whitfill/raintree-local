# AI Readiness Assessment — Zoom Interview Guide
**Raintree Marketing Services** | raintreems.com | For internal use only

*This is YOUR guide for the 45-minute Zoom call. Record the call (with client permission).*
*You don't send this to the client — just run through these questions conversationally.*
*Goal: get them talking. The more they describe their day, the better Claude's report.*

---

## Before the Call (5 min)

- Confirm recording consent: *"Is it okay if I record this call? It helps me make sure I capture everything accurately."*
- Set expectations: *"This is about 45 minutes. I'm just going to ask you questions about how your business runs day-to-day. No right or wrong answers — the more specific you are, the better I can help you."*
- Note their business name, type, city, team size, and website before the call so you don't waste time on basics.

---

## Section 1: Business Overview (5 min)

1. Tell me what your business does and who your typical customer is.
2. How big is your team right now — employees, contractors, virtual assistants?
3. What tools or software does your team use every day? *(Let them list — don't prompt. Common ones: email, QuickBooks, scheduling tools, CRM, Google Workspace, etc.)*
4. Are you using any AI tools right now — ChatGPT, Claude, anything like that?

---

## Section 2: How Work Actually Gets Done (10 min)

5. Walk me through a typical workday. What does your morning look like before lunch?
6. What's the first thing you do when a new lead or customer inquiry comes in?
7. How quickly do you usually respond — and what happens if you miss one?
8. What does your customer onboarding or intake process look like? Is it consistent or does it vary?
9. How do you track what's happening with each customer or project? *(Spreadsheet? CRM? Their head?)*
10. Do you create any recurring reports — for yourself, your team, or anyone else? How long does that take?

---

## Section 3: Where Time Gets Lost (10 min)

11. What's the most time-consuming thing you do every week that you wish you didn't have to do personally?
12. What tasks do you or your team do over and over that feel like they should just happen automatically?
13. Are there things that fall through the cracks — customer follow-ups, reviews, scheduling — that you know you should be doing but don't have time for?
14. What's something you'd do more of if you had 5 extra hours a week?
15. Have you ever tried to automate something or buy a tool to solve a problem, and it didn't work out? What happened?

---

## Section 4: Customer Communication (10 min)

16. What are the 3–5 questions customers ask you most often — the ones you feel like you answer a hundred times?
17. What happens when a customer calls or emails after hours?
18. How do you handle customer follow-ups after a job or sale is complete?
19. Do you have a system for getting reviews, or does it happen on its own?
20. What's the biggest communication frustration you have — with customers, leads, or your team?

---

## Section 5: Pain Points & Goals (10 min)

21. If you had to pick one thing that's costing you the most time or money right now, what is it?
22. What does your business look like 12 months from now if things go really well?
23. What's the thing that's most in the way of getting there?
24. Have you looked into AI before? What's your honest take on it — excited, skeptical, confused?
25. Is there anything I haven't asked about that you think I should know?

---

## Closing the Call (2 min)

- *"This is really helpful. I have everything I need to put together your report."*
- *"You'll have your AI Readiness Assessment in your inbox within 48 hours."*
- *"It'll include the specific tools and changes I'd recommend for your situation, plus a financial impact estimate of what implementing them could save you."*
- *"After you've had a chance to review it, we'll hop on a 30-minute follow-up call to walk through everything. Sound good?"*
- Confirm their email address before ending.

---

## After the Call

1. Stop recording — download the transcript (Zoom auto-transcribes, or use Otter.ai / Fathom)
2. Clean up the transcript lightly — remove filler words, fix obvious transcription errors
3. Open Claude and use the **AI Assessment Claude Prompt** (see ai-assessment-claude-prompt.md)
4. Paste the cleaned transcript into the CLIENT TRANSCRIPT section
5. Claude generates the report
6. Review it — correct anything that doesn't match what you heard on the call
7. Format in Google Doc or Gamma using the Raintree Marketing Services template
8. Send within 48 hours with subject: `Your AI Readiness Assessment — [Business Name]`
