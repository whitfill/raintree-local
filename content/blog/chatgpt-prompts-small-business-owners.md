The small business owners who get the most value from ChatGPT aren't using it to do anything complicated. They're using it to eliminate the blank page — to get a first draft in 30 seconds instead of spending 45 minutes writing something from scratch.

The key is giving ChatGPT enough context. A bad prompt produces a generic, useless response. A good prompt produces something you can actually use with minor edits.

Here are 25 prompts organized by use case. Copy, adjust the details in brackets, and use.

## Customer Communication

**1. Follow-up email to an unresponsive lead**
> "Write a short, friendly follow-up email to a potential customer who got a quote from us 5 days ago and hasn't responded. We're a [type of business] in [city, Texas]. Keep it warm, not pushy. End with an easy way to move forward."

**2. Thank-you email after a completed job**
> "Write a short thank-you email to send after completing a job for a customer. We're a [type of business]. Include a brief mention that we'd appreciate a Google review if they were happy with the work, and include this link: [your review link]."

**3. Response to a positive Google review**
> "Write a warm, genuine response to this Google review: [paste the review]. My business is [business name], a [type of business] in [city]. Don't sound like a PR statement — sound like a real person."

**4. Response to a negative Google review**
> "Write a professional, empathetic response to this negative Google review: [paste the review]. Don't get defensive. Acknowledge the concern, briefly address it, and invite them to contact us directly to resolve it. My business is [business name]."

**5. Booking confirmation email**
> "Write a booking confirmation email for a customer who just scheduled a [type of appointment] with us. Include: the date and time, what to expect, what to bring (if anything), and our contact info. Business: [business name], [city, TX], phone: [number]."

**6. Customer re-engagement email (hasn't visited in a while)**
> "Write a short, friendly email to a customer who visited our [type of business] about 6 months ago and hasn't been back. Don't be needy — be genuine. Let them know we've got new [inventory/services] and would love to see them again."

## Social Media

**7. Instagram caption for a product photo**
> "Write 3 Instagram caption options for a photo of [describe the product or scene]. My business is a [type of business] in [city, Texas]. Tone: warm and knowledgeable, not sales-y. Include a call to action and suggest relevant hashtags."

**8. Instagram caption for a behind-the-scenes photo**
> "Write an Instagram caption for a behind-the-scenes photo of [describe what's happening — sourcing trip, booth setup, restoration in progress, etc.]. My business: [type of business]. Keep it personal and conversational."

**9. Monthly GBP post series**
> "Write 4 Google Business Profile posts — one per week for the month of [month]. My business is [type of business] in [city, Texas]. Mix in: one product/service highlight, one behind-the-scenes, one customer-focused, one timely/seasonal. Keep each post to 3 sentences max."

**10. Pinterest pin description**
> "Write a Pinterest pin description for a photo of [describe the image]. My business is [type of business] in [city, Texas]. Include relevant search terms buyers would use when looking for this type of product or planning a trip to [city]. Keep it natural — no keyword stuffing."

## Operations and Admin

**11. FAQ content for your website**
> "Write answers to these 5 frequently asked questions for my business: [list your FAQs]. My business is [business name], a [type of business] in [city, Texas]. Write each answer in plain language, 2-4 sentences, from the perspective of the business owner."

**12. Service description for your GBP or website**
> "Write a clear, specific description of this service for our Google Business Profile and website: [describe the service]. My business is [business name]. Keep it to 150-200 words. Focus on what the customer gets, not just what we do."

**13. Job posting**
> "Write a job posting for a [position title] at my [type of business] in [city, Texas]. We're looking for [2-3 key qualities]. The role involves [briefly describe main responsibilities]. Pay is [range or 'competitive']. We're a small team and [one thing that makes working here good]."

**14. Employee onboarding checklist**
> "Write a simple first-week onboarding checklist for a new [position] at a [type of business]. Include: things to learn, people to meet, tasks to shadow, and a few questions to answer by the end of the first week."

**15. Proposal or estimate follow-up**
> "Write a follow-up message to send 3 days after sending a proposal or estimate that hasn't been responded to. I'm a [type of business] in Texas. Keep it short — I just want to check in and make it easy for them to respond with questions."

## Marketing and Sales

**16. Elevator pitch for your business**
> "Write a clear, concise 3-sentence description of my business for use in marketing materials, a website about page, and networking conversations. Business: [business name]. We do: [what you do]. Our typical customers are: [describe them]. What makes us different: [your differentiator]."

**17. Email newsletter intro**
> "Write an intro paragraph for a monthly email newsletter for [business name], a [type of business] in [city, Texas]. The theme for this month is [theme or topic]. Keep it conversational and warm — this goes to customers who already know us."

**18. Business bio for a directory listing**
> "Write a 150-word business bio for our [directory or platform] listing. Business: [business name], [type of business], located in [city, Texas]. We've been in business [X years]. We specialize in [specific thing]. Our customers are [describe them]."

**19. Seasonal promotion announcement**
> "Write a short promotional message for [platform: email / Instagram / Facebook] announcing [describe the promotion] for [season or occasion]. My business is [type]. Keep it enthusiastic but not over-the-top. Include the offer details clearly."

**20. Review request text message**
> "Write a short, friendly text message asking a recent customer to leave a Google review. My business is [business name] in [city]. Include a place to insert the review link. Keep it to 2-3 sentences — feels personal, not automated."

## Planning and Strategy

**21. Monthly content calendar**
> "Create a simple content calendar for [month] for my [type of business] in [city, Texas]. I post on Instagram and Google Business Profile. Include: 3 Instagram posts per week and 1 GBP post per week. Give me a theme or topic for each one based on what's seasonal or relevant for [month] in Texas."

**22. Competitor analysis prompt**
> "I want to understand what [competitor business name] is doing well online. They're a [type of business] in [city, Texas]. Based on what you know about local business digital marketing, what are the most likely things they're doing well if they outrank me on Google? What should I look at?"

**23. Business process SOP**
> "Write a simple standard operating procedure for this process: [describe the process — customer intake, job completion, review request, etc.]. Format it as a numbered checklist that a new employee could follow without help. Keep each step to one sentence."

**24. Goal-setting prompt**
> "Help me set 3 specific, measurable digital marketing goals for my [type of business] in [city, Texas] over the next 90 days. My current situation: [briefly describe where you are — how many reviews, whether you're in the local pack, current social following, etc.]. Goals should be realistic and tied to business outcomes."

**25. Diagnosis prompt when something isn't working**
> "My [business type] in [city, Texas] isn't showing up in Google Maps searches for [target keywords]. What are the most common reasons a business in this situation wouldn't rank in the local pack, and what would you recommend checking first?"

---

## One Thing to Remember

ChatGPT's output is a first draft, not a final product. Always read it, adjust the details that don't match your situation, and add one or two things that are specific to your actual business or the actual customer you're writing to. The goal is to save the blank-page time — not to publish AI content verbatim.
